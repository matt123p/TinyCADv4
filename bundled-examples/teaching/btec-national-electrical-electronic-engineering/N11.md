## Compare two voltages

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The comparator output changes state according to which input voltage is greater. Its output is limited by its supply rails.

### Your investigation

Predict the switching points. Change the reference and compare the input and output traces.

### This activity

Sweep one input across a reference and capture output switching. Include supply limits and a suitable comparator/op-amp model.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.
