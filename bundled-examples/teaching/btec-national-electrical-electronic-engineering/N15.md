## Inputs and truth tables

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

All gates use the same labelled input signals. Equal net labels mean an electrical connection even where no long wire is drawn.

### Your investigation

List every input combination and predict each output before running. Compare the waveforms with your truth tables.

### This activity

Test all input combinations of the specified basic gates. State logic levels, supply and the selected device family.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
