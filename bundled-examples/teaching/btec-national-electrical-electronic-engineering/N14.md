## Gain depends on frequency

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The capacitor provides a frequency-dependent feedback path. At higher frequencies it reduces the feedback impedance and the closed-loop gain.

### Your investigation

Predict the corner frequency from Rf and C. Use the AC plot to compare gain and phase below, near and above that frequency.

### This activity

Supply a model with frequency dependence. Compare cutoff and gain–bandwidth behaviour; use a declared active filter topology if investigating its component sensitivity.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.
