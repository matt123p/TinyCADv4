## From a circuit to a real assembly

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

A schematic describes electrical connections. The same connections can be arranged in many physical layouts.

### Your investigation

Trace SUPPLY through the switch, limiting resistor and LED to RETURN. Mark polarity and test points, then sketch a practical layout and have it checked.

### This activity

Annotate supplies, polarity, pin names and test points. The schematic supports construction and measurement; it does not prove safe soldering, assembly quality or the learner's practical performance.

### Getting started

This is a connection-planning drawing. Follow each labelled net, identify the real terminals and add your bench measurements. No simulation is attached.
