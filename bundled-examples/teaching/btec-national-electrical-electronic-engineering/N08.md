## Control a load with gate voltage

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The load current is controlled by the gate-to-source voltage. The source is the reference for the gate drive.

### Your investigation

Compare the gate waveform and drain voltage. Explain the role of the gate pull resistor.

### This activity

Compare electrical load response to gate pulses and input-voltage changes. Use the appropriate N/P-channel model and connections.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
