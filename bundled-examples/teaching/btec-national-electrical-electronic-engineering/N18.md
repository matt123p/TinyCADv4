## A level and an edge are different

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

A latch can follow its input while enabled. A flip-flop samples at a clock edge and then holds its state.

### Your investigation

Compare DATA, CLOCK and Q. Change DATA away from the sampling edge and explain what each device stores.

### This activity

Apply defined clock/data/reset sequences to prepared R-S, D or JK models. Capture state changes and distinguish asynchronous inputs from clocked inputs.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
