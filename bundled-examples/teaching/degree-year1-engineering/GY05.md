## From electrical power to energy

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

Electrical power can be found from voltage times current. Energy is power accumulated over time.

### Your investigation

Calculate power in each resistance and compare the total with the source. State what the electrical model cannot tell you about real temperature.

### This activity

Account for resistor, source and load power for different operating conditions. Distinguish predicted electrical dissipation from temperature rise or mechanical output.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
