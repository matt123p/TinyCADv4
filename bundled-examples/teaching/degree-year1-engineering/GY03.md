## Replace a network by an equivalent source

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

A source network can be represented by an open-circuit voltage and an equivalent resistance as viewed from its load terminals.

### Your investigation

Measure the load voltage for several loads. Compare the source equivalent with the original network.

### This activity

Compare an original network with its port equivalent for several loads. State source resistance and avoid treating an ideal source as a complete battery model.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.

R1 represents the source resistance. V1 is the ideal emf. R2 is the removable load. With R2 removed, derive the open-circuit voltage from R1 and R3; suppress V1 to find R1 in parallel with R3. Compare the original with its equivalent under several loads.

**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
