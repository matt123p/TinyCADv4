## Gain depends on frequency

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

The capacitor provides a frequency-dependent feedback path. At higher frequencies it reduces the feedback impedance and the closed-loop gain.

### Your investigation

Predict the corner frequency from Rf and C. Use the AC plot to compare gain and phase below, near and above that frequency.

### This activity

Compare the passive and buffered/active versions of a selected filter under changed loading. Use a model with adequate bandwidth.
