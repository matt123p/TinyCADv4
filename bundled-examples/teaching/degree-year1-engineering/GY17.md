## Measure a small imbalance

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

A small resistance change unbalances the bridge. Its two sense voltages are nearly equal, so a difference amplifier helps reveal the small signal.

### Your investigation

Calculate both bridge midpoint voltages. Predict the amplifier output, then change Arm1 above and below 1000 ohms. Keep the two sense connections separate.

### This activity

Supply measured sensor voltages/resistances and investigate offset, loading and conditioning. Do not claim the circuit independently reproduces the physical sensing mechanism.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.

Arm1 = 1001 ohms; the other bridge arms = 1000 ohms. Both sense terminals are free to move. The matched resistor ratios give OUT = 10 × (PLUS − MINUS).
