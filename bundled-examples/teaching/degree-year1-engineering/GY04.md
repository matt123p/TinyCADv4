## Separate the effects of two sources

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

In a linear circuit the voltage or current due to both independent sources is the sum of their separate contributions.

### Your investigation

Run with both sources. Set one voltage source to zero at a time, retaining its series resistance, and add the signed results.

### This activity

Investigate each independent source separately, then compare the total voltage/current. Show explicitly why power is not a linear sum of these separate results.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.

V1 = 5 V and V2 = 3 V. To suppress a source set its DC voltage to zero; retain both resistors.
