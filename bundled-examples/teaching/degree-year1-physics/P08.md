## Energy exchanged between L and C

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

The inductor and capacitor exchange stored energy. Resistance dissipates that energy and damps the oscillation.

### Your investigation

Estimate the natural frequency. Compare several resistance values and classify the resulting damping.

### This activity

Change R and compare oscillatory and non-oscillatory responses. Extract a decay rate and oscillation period from an adequately sampled transient.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
