## Magnitude and phase

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

With a sinusoidal input, resistance and reactance affect both the size and timing of voltage and current.

### Your investigation

Use the AC analysis to compare magnitude and phase. Check your result with complex impedance.

### This activity

Sweep RC and RL circuits, measure voltage/current phase and compare with phasor calculations. State the reference direction and phase convention.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
