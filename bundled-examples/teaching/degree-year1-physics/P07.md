## An inductor opposes changes in current

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

The coil current cannot jump instantly. Its series resistance sets the final current and dissipates energy.

### Your investigation

Predict L/R. Compare the voltage across the inductor with the rate at which the current changes.

### This activity

Apply a step to a resistive-inductive circuit, include coil resistance and compare fitted time constant with the model prediction. A lumped inductor does not model the magnetic geometry.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
