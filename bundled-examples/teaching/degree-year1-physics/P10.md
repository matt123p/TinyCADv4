## Find a resonant frequency

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

In a series RLC network, the inductive and capacitive reactances cancel at resonance. Resistance controls the peak current and the bandwidth.

### Your investigation

Predict 1/(2π√(LC)). Sweep frequency, inspect source current and compare the resonant frequency with your prediction. Change R and explain the bandwidth.

### This activity

Sweep an RLC circuit, measure response and bandwidth, then compare the inferred resonance with the transient experiment. Include generator output impedance.
