# First-year electronics degree — representative circuit/device/digital strand

Student circuit activities following 25-first-year-degree-electronics-experiments.md. Each drawing contains a Markdown guide with a prediction, investigation and explanation task.

Open a simulation (.dzn), select its **Simulation** tab and press **Run**. Ordinary drawings (.dsn) are connection plans for physical work, measurements or programming. Equal net labels mean an electrical connection.

These are configurable educational models: compare their assumptions with the real components and equipment used in class. Save your changes as your own copy.
