## A one-bit addition

**First-year degree · ages 18+**

A full adder combines A, B and carry-in. Its sum and carry-out represent a two-bit result.

### Your investigation

Write all eight input combinations. Predict SUM and CARRY_OUT and compare them with the transient signals.

### This activity

Implement a small gate-level arithmetic example and enumerate operand combinations. Explain signed/unsigned interpretation outside the circuit.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
