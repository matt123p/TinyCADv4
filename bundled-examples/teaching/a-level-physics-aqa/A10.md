## Two cells, one supply

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

Each cell contributes an emf. In series the emfs add; real cells also add internal resistance. Identical cells in parallel share load current.

### Your investigation

Predict the loaded voltage for the series pair. Build a separate parallel copy with a resistor in each cell branch, then compare under the same load.

### This activity

Include an internal resistance for each source. Compare terminal voltage and load current for the same load; avoid modelling parallel cells as contradictory ideal voltage sources.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.

Start with two 1.5 V cells in series. R1 = 200 ohms represents their combined internal resistance (100 ohms each). For the parallel comparison, give each cell its own 100-ohm series resistance before joining the terminals; never place different ideal source voltages directly in parallel.

**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
