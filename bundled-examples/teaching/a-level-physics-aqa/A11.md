## Choose a fraction of a voltage

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The wiper selects a point along the resistance. A connected load can change the voltage it produces.

### Your investigation

Change Wiper between 0 and 1. Compare the unloaded ratio with the loaded output.

### This activity

Use two resistors, or two resistor segments representing a potentiometer. Vary the ratio and compare unloaded output with output connected to a finite load.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
