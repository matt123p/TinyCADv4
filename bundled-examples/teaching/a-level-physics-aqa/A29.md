## Add signals with feedback

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

Each input resistor contributes a current to the inverting input node. Feedback converts the sum of those currents into an output voltage.

### Your investigation

Predict the signed contribution of each input. Change one input at a time, then compare the combined result.

### This activity

Apply two independently adjustable inputs through different resistors. Compare the measured output with a weighted sum, then investigate clipping with a suitable model.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
