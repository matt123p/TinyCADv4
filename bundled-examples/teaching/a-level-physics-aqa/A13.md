## From electrical power to energy

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

Electrical power can be found from voltage times current. Energy is power accumulated over time.

### Your investigation

Calculate power in each resistance and compare the total with the source. State what the electrical model cannot tell you about real temperature.

### This activity

Measure delivered load power and power in an explicit source resistor. Vary the load and account for the electrical energy supplied. Physical cell heating and chemical behaviour need additional models.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
