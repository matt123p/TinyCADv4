## Watching stored charge leave

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The source pulse first charges the capacitor, then returns to zero so that the resistor provides a discharge path.

### Your investigation

Compare the rising and falling parts of OUT. Estimate the time constant and the energy initially stored.

### This activity

Capture decay after a known charging interval, using a suitable switch model or an explicitly stated source arrangement. Export the decay for a plot of ln(V) against time and obtain RC from its gradient. Include every discharge path, including meter resistance.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
