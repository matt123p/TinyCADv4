## Subtract two signals

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

Four matched resistor ratios make a difference amplifier. This arrangement rejects a voltage that is shared by both inputs.

### Your investigation

Predict OUT = SECOND − SIGNAL. Increase both input offsets by the same amount, then deliberately mismatch a resistor and compare.

### This activity

Compare differential excitation with equal voltages on both inputs. Deliberately change one resistor to investigate imperfect common-mode rejection.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
