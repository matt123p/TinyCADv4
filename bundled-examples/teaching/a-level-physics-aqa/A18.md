## Reading a waveform

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The source changes smoothly between positive and negative values. Its peak value, peak-to-peak value and RMS value describe different measurements.

### Your investigation

Read the period and calculate the frequency. Compare the peaks with the expected RMS value of a sine wave.

### This activity

Capture complete cycles and compare a scope's voltage readout, AC meter reading and calculated RMS. Use a zero-offset sine initially; distinguish AC-only RMS from total RMS when adding an offset.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
