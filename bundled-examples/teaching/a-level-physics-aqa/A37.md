## Restart a count at five

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The binary counter feeds an AND gate from Q0 and Q2. When the count reaches five, the decoded signal resets the counter.

### Your investigation

Write the expected 0–4 sequence. Plot Q0, Q1, Q2 and MOD_RESET, then explain why the state five is brief. Change the decode for a different modulus.

### This activity

Add reset-decoding logic to a counter and verify the sequence length. Use a sufficiently small time step to capture reset pulses and avoid assuming ideal zero-delay behaviour.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.

R1/C1 and the hysteretic voltage-controlled switch hold the decoded reset long enough to clear all counter bits. The reset pulse is short compared with the clock period.
