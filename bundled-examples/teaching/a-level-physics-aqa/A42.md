## A carrier with a changing envelope

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The modulator multiplies the carrier by a changing amplitude term. A non-zero carrier bias retains the carrier.

### Your investigation

Compare carrier, message and output. Change ModulationIndex and identify the onset of overmodulation.

### This activity

Supply a behavioural or compatible modulator model and compare carrier, message and modulated traces. Vary message amplitude/frequency. The proposed circuit implementation is optional; the syllabus does not require its details.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
