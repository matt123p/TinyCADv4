## Amplify without changing polarity

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

Negative feedback makes the inverting input follow the signal at the non-inverting input.

### Your investigation

Predict 1 + Rf/Rg. Compare the two waveforms and check the effect of a larger input.

### This activity

Repeat the gain measurement with a non-inverting configuration, then change the feedback ratio. Make supply rails and signal reference explicit in the diagram.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
