## The measuring device is part of the circuit

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

A voltmeter takes a small current. A current meter adds a small series resistance. A high-resistance circuit makes these effects easier to see.

### Your investigation

Compare the unloaded prediction with the measured circuit. Decide which instrument setting matters most.

### This activity

Predict readings for several declared reference resistors and source values. Draw the real calibration arrangement and compare bench readings against an independently established reference. A virtual meter is not a calibration certificate for physical equipment.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
