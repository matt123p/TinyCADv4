## A filament warms up

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The lamp includes a simplified thermal model. Heating changes its resistance, so its current is not proportional to applied voltage.

### Your investigation

Compare low- and high-voltage points. State the model assumptions before comparing with a real lamp.

### This activity

Draw the current-limited measurement circuit and compare a real lamp characteristic with an electrothermal or empirical model. Keep a fixed-resistance illustration clearly labelled as an approximation.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
