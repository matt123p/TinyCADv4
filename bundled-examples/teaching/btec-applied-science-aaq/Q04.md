## Wire resistance

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

A longer wire of the same material and thickness has more resistance. The resistance here is calculated from declared material and dimensions; it is not a measurement of a real wire.

### Your investigation

Change wire_length, then diameter. Record voltage and current and calculate R = V/I. Keep other quantities unchanged.

### This activity

Draw the test section and meter positions. Enter measured resistances or a stated wire law for each length; plot exported R against length externally.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.

Wire model: rho = 1.1e-6 ohm metre; diameter = 0.3 mm; length = 0.5 m. Change the Simulation-sheet parameters.

**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
