## Connect meters to the right places

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

Voltage is measured across two points. Current is measured by putting the meter into the current path. Never connect a current meter directly across the supply.

### Your investigation

Trace the series current path and identify the voltage connections before running the circuit.

### This activity

Prepare an ammeter/voltmeter test circuit, then compare readings for a known load. Use V Ω–COM for voltage/resistance and A–COM for current.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.

DMM1 measures OUT relative to the return. DMM2 is in the return path: its A jack connects to I_RET and its COM jack to ground.
