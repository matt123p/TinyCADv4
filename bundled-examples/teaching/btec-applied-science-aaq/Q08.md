## An LED needs current limiting

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

An LED conducts mainly in one direction. The series resistor limits its current; the electrical model does not predict visible brightness or damage.

### Your investigation

Estimate a sensible resistor value. Compare the LED current for different values and for reversed polarity.

### This activity

Compare current and resistor dissipation for different supply/resistor choices. Use an LED-specific model; light output and damage are not automatic simulation results.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
