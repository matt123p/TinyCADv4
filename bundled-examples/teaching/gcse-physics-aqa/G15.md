## From electrical power to energy

**GCSE · ages 14–16**

Electrical power can be found from voltage times current. Energy is power accumulated over time.

### Your investigation

Calculate power in each resistance and compare the total with the source. State what the electrical model cannot tell you about real temperature.

### This activity

Measure current and voltage for several resistor/supply combinations. Compare calculated power with a power trace; use a transient capture or a timed steady reading to calculate energy. This predicts electrical dissipation, not temperature rise or visible brightness.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
