## Sharing a voltage

**GCSE · ages 14–16**

Two series resistors share the supply voltage. OUT is measured from their meeting point to the reference node.

### Your investigation

Predict OUT using the resistance ratio, then swap the values. Explain your results.

### This activity

Choose unequal resistors, measure each voltage and compare their sum with the supply. Swap the resistors and predict the new readings before running.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
