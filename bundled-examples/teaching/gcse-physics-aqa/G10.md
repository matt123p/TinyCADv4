## More than one current path

**GCSE · ages 14–16**

Parallel branches share the same pair of nodes, so they share the same voltage. The supply current is the sum of the branch currents.

### Your investigation

Compare the branch currents. Increase one branch resistance and explain what happens to the total current.

### This activity

Use two unequal branch resistors. Measure both branch voltages, both currents and the supply current. Repeat with one branch open.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
