## One current, several resistors

**GCSE · ages 14–16**

In a series path the same current passes through every component. The voltage changes across each resistor add to the supply voltage.

### Your investigation

Predict the current. Run the circuit, then change one resistance and check the prediction.

### This activity

Put current probes before and after different components. Compare readings while changing supply voltage or resistance. Use a short transient capture to connect a steady current with charge transferred over a chosen time.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
