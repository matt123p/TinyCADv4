## A diode has a direction

**GCSE · ages 14–16**

The limiting resistor protects the current path. Forward and reverse operation have very different current scales.

### Your investigation

Plot diode current against the voltage across the diode, not against the whole supply.

### This activity

Use a diode model and series limiting resistor. Sweep the supply in both directions and measure voltage across the diode separately from supply voltage. Compare forward conduction and reverse current; use a graph scale that makes the intended feature visible.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
