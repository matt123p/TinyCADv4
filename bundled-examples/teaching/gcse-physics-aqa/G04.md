## Plot a resistor characteristic

**GCSE · ages 14–16**

The DC sweep changes the source in both directions. Measure the component voltage separately from the source when there is a series resistance.

### Your investigation

Export voltage and current. Plot I against V, include units and estimate the resistance from the gradient.

### This activity

Draw the supply, current-limiting control, ammeter in series and voltmeter across the test resistor. Sweep supply voltage through positive and negative values. Export paired V/I readings for a characteristic graph and a resistance estimate.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
