## Reading a waveform

**GCSE · ages 14–16**

The source changes smoothly between positive and negative values. Its peak value, peak-to-peak value and RMS value describe different measurements.

### Your investigation

Read the period and calculate the frequency. Compare the peaks with the expected RMS value of a sine wave.

### This activity

Compare a DC source with a low-voltage sine source on the scope. Identify polarity changes and repeat at different frequencies. This is an electronic demonstration of the distinction; it does not require a mains-voltage experiment.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
