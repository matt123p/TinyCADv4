## A light-dependent input

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The LDR resistance falls as its declared illumination rises. The resistor position determines whether OUT rises or falls.

### Your investigation

Change Illuminance and record OUT. Explain why distance from a real lamp requires separate calibration.

### This activity

Enter measured sensor resistances for several illumination conditions and predict divider output. Optical distance/intensity must come from measurements or a declared model.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
