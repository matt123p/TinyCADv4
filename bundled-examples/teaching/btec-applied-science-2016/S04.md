## A source has internal resistance

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The source has an emf and a series resistance. The terminal voltage falls as the load takes more current.

### Your investigation

Change the load. Use the terminal-voltage and current readings to estimate the source resistance.

### This activity

Use an ideal source plus a series resistor. Change the load and fit terminal voltage against current using exported values. Identify the declared model's emf and resistance.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.

R1 represents the source resistance. V1 is the ideal emf.

**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
