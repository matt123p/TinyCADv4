## A low-pass filter

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

The capacitor impedance falls as frequency rises. More of the high-frequency signal is diverted to the reference, leaving a smaller OUT voltage.

### Your investigation

Predict the corner frequency 1/(2πRC). Use the AC plot to compare magnitude and phase, then double R or C and explain the shift.

### This activity

Apply a sine or pulse to an RC network. Compare input/output amplitude and timing across frequencies and relate the result to the chosen component values.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
