## A capacitor takes time to charge

**Post-16 · ages 16–19**

Current charges the capacitor through the resistor. OUT rises gradually rather than jumping straight to the source voltage.

### Your investigation

Predict the time constant R × C. Run the transient, then double R or C and compare the curves.

### This activity

Repeat a selected circuit with nominal and measured component values. Keep predicted and bench data separate, then identify discrepancies attributable to loading, tolerance or an inadequate model.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** Use an equation, a truth table or a state sequence to justify your prediction. Include units and discuss the model assumptions.
