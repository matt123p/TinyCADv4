## A timer that keeps switching

**BTEC First · ages 14–16**

The timing capacitor repeatedly charges and discharges between the timer thresholds. Two resistors set different charge and discharge times.

### Your investigation

Measure period and duty cycle. Compare with T ≈ 0.693 × (RA + 2RB) × C, then change one resistor.

### This activity

Capture a timer's output and compare frequency/duty cycle for different component values. A flashing LED's electrical drive can be tested without modelling visual flashing.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
