## A small signal controls a load

**BTEC First · ages 14–16**

The input resistor limits base current. The transistor connects the load to the return rail when its base is driven.

### Your investigation

Compare the input, collector voltage and load current. Check the conditions for a saturated switch.

### This activity

Sweep the input to an NPN or PNP stage driving an electrical load. Compare input, output and device currents. Supply the correct model and pin mapping.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
