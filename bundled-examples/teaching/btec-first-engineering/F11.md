## One trigger, one timed pulse

**BTEC First · ages 14–16**

The trigger input briefly goes low. The timer then charges its timing capacitor until the threshold ends the output pulse.

### Your investigation

Measure the output pulse width and compare it with 1.1 × R × C. Double R, rerun and explain the change.

### This activity

Use a compatible timer model, trigger once and measure pulse duration. Change the timing components. A predefined pulse source cannot demonstrate the timer's timing law.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.



**Explain:** What changed, and why? Use your readings as evidence.

The model predicts electrical behaviour; it does not replace real practical measurements.
