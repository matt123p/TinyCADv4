## Energy exchanged between L and C

**Higher National · level 4**

The inductor and capacitor exchange stored energy. Resistance dissipates that energy and damps the oscillation.

### Your investigation

Estimate the natural frequency. Compare several resistance values and classify the resulting damping.

### This activity

Compare time-domain decay with frequency response for different resistances. Measure resonance, bandwidth and Q using a stated criterion.
