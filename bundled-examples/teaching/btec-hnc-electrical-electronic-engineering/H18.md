## Feedback sets the gain

**Higher National · level 4**

The input signal reaches the inverting input through a resistor. A feedback resistor returns part of the output to the same node.

### Your investigation

Predict the signed gain from the resistor ratio. Check amplitude, inversion and output clipping.

### This activity

Choose a simple inverting/non-inverting stage and compare predicted gain with captures. Supply rail limits and a model adequate to the measurement.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.
