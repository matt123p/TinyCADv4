## A low-pass filter

**Higher National · level 4**

The capacitor impedance falls as frequency rises. More of the high-frequency signal is diverted to the reference, leaving a smaller OUT voltage.

### Your investigation

Predict the corner frequency 1/(2πRC). Use the AC plot to compare magnitude and phase, then double R or C and explain the shift.

### This activity

Compare RC low-pass/high-pass networks with the same source and load. Relate AC response to pulse-shape changes from transient runs.
