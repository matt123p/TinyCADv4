## Match a load to its source

**Higher National · level 4**

Changing the load changes both current and load voltage. The greatest transferred power need not occur at the greatest current.

### Your investigation

Try load values below and above the source resistance. Calculate load power and the efficiency of the transfer.

### This activity

Vary a resistor load against a source with explicit resistance. Compare load power and efficiency; maximum delivered power is not maximum efficiency.

### Getting started

Open the Simulation tab and press Run. Select a source or component to see its values. Read the labelled signals, then change one quantity at a time. Equal net labels identify connected points.

R1 represents the source resistance. V1 is the ideal emf.
